An amateur-tennis project combining sports footage, personal expression preferences and AI-assisted production to help players create videos with an individual character.

College + industry

A three-person college project developed with Chengshi Sports (Alibaba).

My contribution

Choice-system design; underlying technical implementation; exhibition and physical interaction; 16 double-sided portrait cards.

Starting with four players

We interviewed four tennis players about recording, reviewing, editing and sharing - and the version of themselves they wanted their films to hold.

What the interviews changed

Similar footage can carry different expectations. Body data describes an action; a player's account helps us understand what that action means to them.

02 Choices

Enlarge ↗
Read this page

Four decisions describe an expressive direction; sixteen paths make the possibilities visible.

The design turn I introduced

The team initially aimed for fully automated production. I proposed that players' understanding of themselves - and the image they wanted to present - should also enter the generation process.

The choice system

I designed a choice system that organised differences from the interviews into four dimensions. A few decisions describe the expressive direction someone wants this time.

Four expression dimensions

MOTIVATION: For myself or For social media / FOCUS: Technical parameters or Emotional atmosphere / DATA ATTITUDE: Record + archive or Aesthetic expression / SOCIAL MODE: Reserved / defensive or Active connection

Portraits guide expression

The team organised these tendencies into four main portraits, each with four variations: sixteen combinations. A result guides the portrait description, recommended effects and later style arrangement.

Creative participation continues

These combinations begin a creative process. Users express a preference through choices and can still participate at the clip and narrative stages.

03 AI crew

A role-based web prototype makes creative decisions visible, with motion analysis supporting the image.

AI director crew

We designed an AI director-crew interaction prototype with six specialist roles: POSE, SCOUT, SELECT, SCRIPT, VFX and EDIT. They organise analysis, selection, narrative and production.

Human participation

At key points, users can confirm the clips to retain, set an expressive tone, and continue participating or hand the next steps to the system. The roles make production easier to understand and give personal intent a clear place to enter.

My underlying motion analysis

My underlying implementation focused on action analysis and effect processing: body keypoints describe pose, person segmentation supports effects on the body, and motion events organise when effects appear.

Preserve the person

Directly showing the skeleton could strip the image of the real body's texture. I moved it into the analysis layer and applied effects to actual people, strengthening movement while preserving the player's own appearance.

Prototype and implementation

Director crew: interaction prototype. Action analysis and effect processing: implementation and output material. Technical studies and finished films are separate project examples.

04 Physical

Enlarge ↗
Read this page

Four choices become a thread pattern, a portrait card and a material preview.

Wind a path

I translated the choice system into a physical interaction. A thread emerges from one hole; visitors make four decisions across eight copper posts in two rows and four columns, winding a personal pattern.

Find a portrait

I designed sixteen double-sided cards. The front shows a corresponding path; the reverse describes qualities through a short poem and suggests visual effects. Visitors find a card through their pattern.

Layer a preview

Visitors select transparent effect cards and fit their holes over two posts at opposite corners of a Federer tennis image. Stacking and replacing layers reveals different visual combinations.

Card 01 / English excerpt

She does not shine through someone else's template; she makes the whole movement her own.

05 Exhibition

Enlarge ↗
Read this page

An exhibition that visitors could understand, choose, try and watch.

My exhibition work

I was responsible for the exhibition and interaction apparatus, bringing research panels, the film screen, winding area and portrait cards together as one stand. Visitors could understand the theme, choose, try and watch.

Participation sequence

Read the project question; choose a thread path and portrait; try transparent effects; watch the film possibilities.

Material language

White surfaces, orange headings and black type extended the project's visual language. Hanging body models, lines and cards echoed the thread paths on the table, connecting the body, its traces and personal expression in one space.

On site

Visitors could wind threads, find cards, stack transparent layers and watch the films. Five demonstration videos explored different directions, including live action, body contours and comic treatments.

Reflection

I helped bring personal ideas into generation, implemented underlying techniques and turned the choice system into a participatory physical experience.

Design interest

This clarified my design interest: connecting personal expression, AI-assisted creation and interaction so technology responds to a particular person.

Recognition

The project received the course collaboration's sole Best Award and recognition from Chengshi Sports.

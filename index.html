AI-assisted sports films, shaped by personal intent.

A personal sports portrait

Tennis footage holds more than technique. Some players value progress; others want to share a mood or a personal style. We bring these different intentions into the making of a film.

My role

Choice system; technical development; exhibition interaction; 16 double-sided profile cards.

Context

A three-person college project in collaboration with Chengshi Sports (Alibaba).

02 Research

▷ Interview I01▷ Interview I03▷ Interview I04Enlarge ↗
Read this page

Four interviews explored recording, reviewing, editing and sharing - and what each person wanted a sports film to say about them.

A record for myself

I01 mainly kept footage for personal recording and review, and rarely shared it. Design implication: keep a private archive and make sharing optional.

A feeling I want to show

I03 wanted to communicate an attitude and the uplifting quality of playing sport. Design implication: let visual tone express a quality the athlete wants to show.

A choice I want to make

I04 valued judging which moments to keep and taking part in editing. Design implication: ask users to confirm highlights and adjust the direction of the film.

My design decision

Body data + personal intent. The team first aimed for full automation. I proposed that the athlete's own ideas should join body data as creative input. I designed a choice system that later informed the AI director-crew concept.

Research position

These four conversations offered situated design material, rather than a universal model of personality. The interview descriptions above are paraphrases, not verbatim quotations.

03 Choices + AI

▷ Play film demoEnlarge ↗
Read this page

Four choice points, four expression families and sixteen starting profiles - with room to revise the direction.

Four dimensions

Motivation: for myself / for social media. Focus: technique / atmosphere. Data: record and archive / aesthetic expression. Connection: reserved / proactive.

Four families

Emotion Splash, Tactical Matrix, Kinetic Echo and Silent Archive each contain four finer variations. A profile is a starting point that the person can still adjust.

The director crew

POSE tracks the body; SCOUT reads the action; SELECT finds moments; SCRIPT frames the story; VFX shapes the image; EDIT assembles the film. Users can accept suggestions, keep their own highlights, change tone or delegate the next step. The English interface on this page is redrawn from the interaction prototype.

My technical work

I implemented body-keypoint tracking, person segmentation and event-based effect control. A key iteration kept the skeleton as an analytical layer and applied effects to real body footage, preserving the athlete's appearance and movement. The analysis and effects images and the expressive film demo are separate project materials.

04 Physical interaction

Enlarge ↗
Read this page

Wind a path, find a profile card and layer visual effects over a fixed tennis image.

Wind a personal path

One starting hole leads to eight copper posts in two rows and four columns. Visitors choose one post in each column, turning four decisions into a visible path.

Find the matching card

I designed 16 double-sided cards. Each front matches a path; its reverse combines a character poem with recommended visual effects.

Layer visual effects

Visitors choose transparent effect cards and place their holes over two posts on a Federer image. The shared base lets them compare different visual combinations directly.

Card 01 - translated excerpt

She does not shine through someone else's template; she makes the whole movement her own.

05 Exhibition

Enlarge ↗
Read this page

An exhibition built around participation.

My exhibition work

I connected the research display, moving-image screen, winding apparatus and profile cards as one exhibition experience. I organised the installation as a sequence: understand the theme, make a choice, try a visual combination and watch the films.

Material language

White surfaces, orange headings and black type connected the digital and physical parts. Hanging body models, lines and cards echoed the table's thread paths.

My contribution

Choice system + technical implementation + exhibition interaction.

Recognition

The project received the collaboration's sole Best Award and recognition from Chengshi Sports.

My interest

Connect personal expression, AI-assisted creation and physical interaction so that technology responds to a particular person.
